Nevaeh Rochford's AI Reflection ♡

After reading Nesrine Malik’s article, “With ‘AI slop’ distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster,” I agree with some of her points, but I also think there are both positive and negative sides to artificial intelligence. I do not think AI is completely ruining society, but I do think people need to be more responsible with how they use it. As a college student, small business owner, and someone pursuing a career in tech, I use AI almost every day. It has become a very useful tool in my life, especially when it comes to school and running my crochet business. However, I also understand the concerns the article brings up, especially about misinformation, fake content, and people using AI only for profit without caring about others.
As a student, AI helps me stay organized and understand information better. Sometimes Google does not give specific enough answers, especially when I have detailed questions about homework or studying. AI helps me create homework checklists, study guides for tests, estimate my grades, and improve my grammar in essays and assignments. I tend to ask a lot of questions while learning, so having AI available feels like having an extra study tool that can explain things in different ways. I do not use it to avoid learning, but more to support my learning. Since I am pursuing a career in technology and web development, I think it is important to understand AI and learn how to work with it instead of pretending it does not exist.
At the same time, I understand why the author is concerned about AI-generated content online. There is so much fake content now that it can become difficult to tell what is real and what is not. Social media is full of AI images, videos, and misinformation that spread quickly because people repost things without checking them. I especially agree with the article’s point about how overwhelming the internet has become. Sometimes it feels like people are constantly consuming content without thinking critically about it. AI can definitely distort reality if it is used irresponsibly.
As a crochet artist and small business owner, AI has also been both helpful and frustrating. On the positive side, AI helps me optimize product descriptions for my website, organize ideas, and create content calendars for social media. It saves time and helps me manage my business more efficiently. However, being on Etsy has shown me the downside of AI and mass production. Small handmade businesses now have to compete with dropshipping companies and print-on-demand businesses that can produce products much faster and cheaper. A lot of handmade crochet pieces and patterns are copied, mass-produced overseas, and sold for prices that real artists cannot compete with. Customers sometimes compare handmade art to these cheap products without understanding the time, effort, and creativity that go into actual crochet work. Losing customers because people would rather buy the cheapest option can be very discouraging.
The part of the article that stood out to me the most was the concern about the future. AI is improving extremely fast, and while it creates opportunities, it is also replacing jobs at an alarming rate. Since I want to work in tech, that idea is both exciting and scary. AI can help developers and businesses become more efficient, but there is still uncertainty about how many careers will eventually be affected. Overall, I believe AI is a powerful tool that can be very beneficial when used correctly, but society still needs to be careful about how dependent we become on it. Only time will tell whether AI becomes something that mainly helps humanity or harms it.
